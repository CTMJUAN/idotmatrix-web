Run without the phone experimental

Draws every minute in advance into an animated GIF timed to the clock, so the display keeps time on its own after you disconnect.

"Start on display" runs the clock live from this page, so keep the page open and the phone connected (the screen stays on automatically). To have it keep running after you disconnect, use "Run without the phone".

Text

"Show on display" keeps the text running after your phone disconnects. To keep messages through a power-off as well, add them to the saved list and write it to the display's memory.

Saved on display

    Writing replaces whatever is currently saved in the display's memory, including items saved from the official app. The display then rotates through the list by itself, even after a power cut.
